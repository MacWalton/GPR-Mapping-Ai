name=GPR Service Mapper
qgisMinimumVersion=3.22
description=Interprets ground-penetrating radar (GSSI .DZT, Sensors & Software .DT1) to map underground services as GIS layers.
about=Reads raw GPR survey lines with their GNSS positions (in the coordinate system they were captured in), processes them, detects pipe and cable hyperbolas, estimates depth and site velocity, traces services across lines, and loads styled survey lines, detections and services (GeoPackage + Shapefile, in the output coordinate system you choose) into the project with an HTML interpretation report. Results are decision support for a competent locator, not a substitute for verification.
